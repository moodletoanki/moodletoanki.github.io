Chrome & Firefox Extension

Moodle to Anki Exporter

Turn completed Moodle quiz reviews (/mod/quiz/review.php) into ready-to-study Anki .apkg decks in one click—100% locally in your browser.

  • No manual copy-paste: Extracts question text, A/B/C/D options, true/false, matching, short answers, and instructor feedback.
  • Images & MathJax intact: Downloads university images using your active session and keeps TeX math expressions clean for Anki.
  • Multi-page quizzes: Automatically pulls full multi-page attempts via Moodle's showall=1 or pagination links.
  • Privacy first: Zero external servers. Your university quiz content never leaves your computer.
