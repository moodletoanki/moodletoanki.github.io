What it handles cleanly

Real .apkg packages, not broken CSV

Builds a valid SQLite collection.anki2 database and media manifest locally so you double-click once and Anki imports everything.

Authenticated Moodle images

Moodle stores diagrams behind login-protected pluginfile.php URLs. The extension fetches them using your active browser session and embeds them inside the .apkg.

MathJax & TeX preservation

Extracts original TeX from rendered MathJax v2/v3 shells into native Anki \(...\) and \[...\] delimiters instead of broken HTML spans.

Multi-page & hidden-key fallback

Automatically loads multi-page reviews (showall=1 or pagination). If an instructor disables .rightanswer, click A/B/C/D right inside the side panel before export.

Multilingual Moodle themes

Relies on core Moodle DOM classes (.que, .qtext, .answer) across custom themes and supports English, Polish, German, Spanish, and French answer labels.

Post-quiz study only

Designed specifically for reviewing completed attempts and past exams. Uses minimal permissions (activeTab, scripting, sidePanel) with zero background tracking.

FAQ

Can my university or instructor see that I exported a quiz?

No. The extension only reads the already-loaded HTML DOM in your browser after clicking Scan (and fetches quiz images or showall=1 pages using standard browser requests, identical to clicking pages normally).

Is any quiz content uploaded to your servers?

No. All HTML parsing, image bundling, SQLite database creation, and ZIP packaging run 100% locally in your browser. See the Privacy Policy.

What if a custom university Moodle theme breaks parsing?

Open an issue via the Support page with an anonymized sample of the .que HTML block, or request a full refund within 14 days under our Refund Policy.
