Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Moodle to Anki Exporter operates entirely locally inside your web browser.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your university quiz questions, answers, grades, Moodle session cookies, browsing history, or personal identity on any external server.

2. Local Processing & Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click the extension icon and trigger a scan on the current Moodle quiz review page to read question DOM elements and fetch embedded quiz images directly in your browser.
  • sidePanel: Displays the interactive preview before generating your .apkg deck.
  • downloads: Saves the locally built .apkg file to your Downloads folder.
  • storage: Stores your UI language preference, export counter, and optional Pro license key locally in your browser profile.

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you choose to enter a Pro license key, only the license key string is sent to the Polar.sh License Validation API (https://api.polar.sh) to verify activation status. No quiz content or Moodle URLs are included in that request.

4. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the support email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
