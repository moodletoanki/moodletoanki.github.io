Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Product: Quiz Exporter for Moodle & Anki

1. Local-Only Architecture

Quiz Exporter for Moodle & Anki runs 100% locally inside your browser. All quiz parsing, image bundling, SQLite database creation (collection.anki2), and .apkg packaging execute strictly on your device.

We do not operate any backend server for quiz content, and we do not collect, log, store, sell, or transmit your Moodle URLs, session cookies, quiz questions, answers, grades, or identity. There are zero analytics or tracking scripts.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose
activeTab & scripting Triggered solely when you click the extension and run a scan on the current Moodle quiz review tab (/mod/quiz/review.php) to read question blocks and fetch embedded images using your active session.
downloads Saves the locally generated .apkg file directly to your device.
storage Stores your UI language preference, local export count, and optional Pro license key locally in chrome.storage.local.

3. Pro License Verification & Payments (Polar.sh)

Free mode requires no account and makes zero external network requests. If you purchase a Pro license ($5.99 one-time), payments and VAT/tax compliance are processed by Polar.sh as Merchant of Record. When you activate a key inside the extension, only the license key string and an anonymous device label are sent to https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/activate. No Moodle content or URLs are included.

4. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (Limited Use)

The use of information received from Google APIs or extension permissions adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Page data accessed on a quiz review tab is used solely to generate your local Anki deck and is never transferred to third parties.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

Uninstall the extension at any time to wipe all local preferences. For questions or support, open an issue at GitHub Issues or email moodletoanki.help@gmail.com.
