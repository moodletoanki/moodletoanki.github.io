Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Product: Moodle to Anki Exporter (Browser Extension)

1. Overview & Local-Only Architecture

Moodle to Anki Exporter is built with a strict local-first architecture. All quiz parsing, image bundling, SQLite database creation (collection.anki2), and .apkg archive packaging happen exclusively inside your browser memory.

We do not operate any backend database for quiz content, and we do not collect, log, store, sell, or share your Moodle URLs, session cookies, quiz questions, answers, grades, or personal identity. The extension uses zero third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

The extension requests only minimal, user-triggered permissions (no broad <all_urls> host access):

Permission Purpose
activeTab & scripting Invoked strictly when you click the extension icon and press Scan Active Moodle Tab. Reads quiz question blocks (.que) from the active page, optionally fetches paginated review pages (showall=1), and fetches embedded quiz images from your active session so they can be packaged offline into the .apkg file.
sidePanel Renders the interactive preview panel where you select questions, review math/answers, and trigger download without closing on background clicks.
downloads Saves the locally generated .apkg flashcard deck directly to your device's Downloads folder.
storage Stores your selected UI language (EN/PL), local export counter, and optional Pro license key locally in chrome.storage.local.

3. Optional Pro License Verification & Payments (Polar.sh)

Using the Free tier requires no account and performs zero network requests outside your Moodle tab. If you choose to purchase or activate a Pro license key:

  • Merchant of Record: Purchases, tax/VAT compliance, receipts, and payment processing are handled by Polar.sh (via Stripe). We never receive or store your credit card or banking details.
  • License Activation Request: When you paste a license key in the extension's Pro panel and click Activate, the extension sends only the license key string and an anonymous extension label over HTTPS to Polar's Customer Portal API (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/activate). No Moodle pages, quiz text, or university data are ever sent in that request.

4. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (Limited Use)

The use of information received from Google APIs or browser extension permissions will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed on a Moodle quiz review tab is used solely to provide the user-facing flashcard export functionality on that device and is never transferred to third parties.

5. Data Retention & GDPR Rights

Because quiz data is never transmitted to our servers, there is no remote personal data to erase. You can remove all locally stored extension preferences and cached license status at any time by clearing extension storage or uninstalling the extension from your browser. For purchase or receipt records handled by Polar.sh, you can manage your order through the Polar customer portal link in your purchase confirmation email.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, bug reports, or support, open an issue at github.com/moodletoanki/moodletoanki.github.io/issues or email moodletoanki@gmail.com.
